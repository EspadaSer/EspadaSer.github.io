First simulation of the power flow

To help understand the power flows of a single-kite ground station, I built a simplified simulation in MATLAB/Simulink. It helped me understand how the electrical devices worked and what values to expect during testing.

The simulation was not intended to show every element of the ground station; it focused on the main ones: the electrical synchronous motor, the DC bus with battery storage, the grid/generator and the braking resistor. The inverter/rectifier, for example, was not modelled because the goal was to study how power flows through the system itself.

The input was a square wave, which is not fully realistic but sufficient for the initial purpose. A second simulation was later run with more elements and with the different kinds of energy flowing through them.

About SwissKitePower

The SwissKitePower project is a multidisciplinary research and development effort aimed at creating a totally new device which can harness wind energy at high altitudes. The winds at altitude are stronger and more consistent than those at the ground and represent an enormous potential source of renewable energy.
