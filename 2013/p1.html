Overhead Power Line

An overhead power line is a structure used in electric power transmission and distribution to carry electrical energy over long distances. The purpose of this project was to develop a methodology for designing an overhead power line that could be scaled to any system.

What made this method particular is that it considered a group of SwissKitePower systems as power generators, similar to a wind farm. A real location in Spain was chosen for the generators and the line, which means Spanish regulations must be met.

The aim was not to design the farm itself, but the connection to the grid. After defining the characteristics of the system, the project focused on how energy would be delivered: an overhead power line carrying power from the farm substation to the grid company’s substation.

About SwissKitePower

The SwissKitePower project is a multidisciplinary research and development effort aimed at creating a totally new device which can harness wind energy at high altitudes. The winds at altitude are stronger and more consistent than those at the ground and represent an enormous potential source of renewable energy.
