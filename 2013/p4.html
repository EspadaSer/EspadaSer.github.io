Validation

To validate the simulation against the real GS1 ground station, I took numerous measurements. Some were taken on individual components, but most were taken directly on the ground station as a whole.

These measurements made it possible to compare the simulated power flows with the real behaviour of the system and to check where the main losses occurred.

About SwissKitePower

The SwissKitePower project is a multidisciplinary research and development effort aimed at creating a totally new device which can harness wind energy at high altitudes. The winds at altitude are stronger and more consistent than those at the ground and represent an enormous potential source of renewable energy.
